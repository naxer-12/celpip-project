01

What is the CELPIP exam and who needs it?

CELPIP (Canadian English Language Proficiency Index Program) is a fully computer-based English language test accepted by IRCC for Canadian permanent residence and citizenship, and by some professional licensing bodies. It tests four skills: Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking, all in one sitting. Each skill is reported as a CELPIP level from 0 to 12, and from level 3 up that level is your CLB level. Anyone applying for Canadian permanent residency, citizenship, or certain professional designations needs a valid CELPIP or equivalent score.

What CLB score do I need for Canadian immigration?

The required CLB score depends on your immigration program. Express Entry (Federal Skilled Worker) requires a minimum of CLB 7 in all four skills. Canadian citizenship requires CLB 4 in Listening and Speaking for applicants aged 18 to 54. Provincial Nominee Programs set their own minimums, which vary by province and stream. For Express Entry points, CLB 9 earns much more than CLB 7 or 8, and CLB 10 or higher earns the most. CELTESTPIP includes sample responses for every CLB level from 4 to 12.

Both are accepted for Canadian immigration. Here is how they differ.

CELPIP

  • Fully computer-based
  • Canadian English, everyday topics
  • All four skills in one sitting

IELTS

  • Paper or computer
  • International English
  • Speaking face to face with an examiner
