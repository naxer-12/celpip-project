Which One Should You Choose?

Neither is universally better. They suit different people, so here is the honest split.

Choose HZAD Education if

  • You want to be taught the strategies step by step on video before you practise anything.
  • Reusable Writing and Speaking templates with worked sample answers are the thing you are missing.
  • You prefer one fixed payment over a subscription, and six months is long enough for your test date.

Choose CELTESTPIP if

  • You already know the strategies and need volume: thousands of scored questions and repeated, timed mock exams.
  • You want instant AI scoring on your Speaking and Writing attempts, so you can revise and resubmit the same evening.
  • You want free courses, a vocabulary trainer, and templates without buying a course first.
  • Your test date is far enough out that ongoing practice matters more than a fixed six-month window.

What HZAD Education Does Well

  • Their course delivers structured, examiner-style video teaching across all four modules, which is genuinely useful if you want to be taught the strategies step by step.
  • They provide reusable Writing and Speaking templates and sample answers that many learners find helpful for the Email and Survey tasks.
  • They advertise a 30-day money-back guarantee, which can lower the risk of trying a paid course.

Many people use both: HZAD Education for a self-paced video course, and CELTESTPIP as their complete practice toolkit: free courses, vocabulary, templates, questions, and mock exams with instant AI feedback. You can start practicing free and decide for yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CELTESTPIP an alternative to the HZAD Education course, or something you use alongside it?+
Both work. HZAD Education is a video course that teaches strategy and templates; CELTESTPIP is a practice platform where you apply those strategies to thousands of scored questions and full mock exams. If you only want to practice, CELTESTPIP is a complete alternative. If you took a course first, CELTESTPIP is the practice layer that turns the lessons into a score.
Does CELTESTPIP include full-length mock exams?+
Yes. CELTESTPIP has 60 full-length, timed mock exams that mirror the real CELPIP format, plus 4,000+ individual practice questions. HZAD's course includes mock tests and unlimited exam simulator access during the course access period, with the count varying by product. They also offer a free AI-marked mock test set separately.
How does the pricing differ?+
HZAD Education prices its course as a one-time payment with six-month access, with no subscriptions. Check their site for current pricing. CELTESTPIP offers flexible plans (weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly) that you can cancel anytime, plus a free tier to start. Which is better value depends on whether you want a fixed course or ongoing, unlimited practice.
Can I try CELTESTPIP for free?+
Yes. You can practice CELPIP questions on CELTESTPIP for free with no credit card, and every question is scored instantly with an explanation. Upgrade only if the extra mocks and AI feedback are helping your score.
