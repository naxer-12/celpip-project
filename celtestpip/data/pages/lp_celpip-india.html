CELPIP guide

What is CELPIP? Full form and meaning

The CELPIP (Canadian English Language Proficiency Index Program) is a fully computer-based English test that IRCC, Canada's immigration department, accepts for permanent residence, Express Entry, the Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) and citizenship. The CELPIP-General tests Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking in one sitting of just under three hours, and each score matches a Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) level.

Canadian PR, Express Entry and the PGWP

Express Entry, the Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP) and the PGWP all accept the CELPIP-General, the version offered at every Indian centre. In 2024, Indian citizens received 43,004 Express Entry invitations, 43% of all 98,903 and the most of any country, and in 2025 about one in four new permanent residents was Indian (98,830 of 393,780).

Canadian citizenship

If you are 18 to 54, citizenship needs proof of CLB 4 in Listening and Speaking. The CELPIP-General results you sent with your PR application count even after they expire, and so does a diploma or transcript from a secondary or post-secondary programme taught in English. The shorter CELPIP-General LS (₹9,500) is offered in India only in Panjim, Goa, and Vadodara, Gujarat.

Australia, but not the UK

Australia's Department of Home Affairs accepts the CELPIP-General taken at a test centre on or after 7 August 2025; an earlier result does not count. UK visas do not accept CELPIP at all.

CELPIP, IELTS or PTE

Is CELPIP easier than IELTS or PTE?

None of the three is officially easier: IRCC converts CELPIP, IELTS and PTE Core results to the same CLB levels, so what differs is the format. CELPIP is fully computer-based, with a single North American accent and recorded Speaking; IELTS has a live Speaking examiner; PTE Core is a two-hour test on a computer. In India, CELPIP is also the cheapest of the three.

CELPIP scores and CLB

CELPIP score chart: the CLB level you need for Canada PR

For Express Entry you need at least CLB 7 in all four skills for the Federal Skilled Worker Program and for Canadian Experience Class TEER 0 or 1 jobs, or CLB 5 for TEER 2 or 3. Each CELPIP score equals the same CLB level (a CELPIP 7 is CLB 7, and the highest is 12), and IRCC checks every skill on its own. On celpip.ca's approximate chart, a 7 takes about 24 to 28 correct answers out of 38 in Reading and 27 to 31 in Listening, and a 9 about 31 to 33 in Reading and 33 to 35 in Listening; the bands can vary slightly from one test to another. How many CRS points is each CLB level worth?

In Express Entry, going from CLB 7 to CLB 9 in all four skills adds 56 language points for an applicant without a spouse (from 68 to 124). Language points stop rising at CLB 10. Skill transferability points also double at CLB 9: for example, language combined with a master's degree (or two post-secondary credentials, one of them three years or longer) rises from 25 to 50 points.

Test format

CELPIP exam pattern and syllabus: four skills in one sitting

The CELPIP-General takes just under three hours, all on a computer, in one sitting: Listening 46 to 55 minutes (38 questions), Reading 43 to 56 minutes (38 questions), Writing 53 minutes (2 tasks) and Speaking 15 minutes (8 tasks). Each part is explained in our CELPIP test format guide.

Tips for Indian candidates, section by section

Listening

Tip: Each recording plays once, and the questions come only after it, so reading the questions ahead, a habit from IELTS, does not work here. Note names, numbers, times and what people decide on the paper the centre gives you, and do not try to catch every word.

Reading

Tip: Candidates often run short of time. For each question, find the part of the passage it points to, choose the option the whole passage supports, and move on. Click an answer for every question before you move on: on many Reading parts you cannot go back.

Writing

Tip: You type both tasks. The screen has a spell-check, but it will not fix a wrong word or a missing a or the. In a friendly email, contractions such as I'm and don't sound natural.

Speaking

Tip: There is no examiner and no face-to-face interview: each of the eight tasks appears on the screen, and you answer out loud into the headset microphone while the computer records you. Other candidates may be talking in the same room, and the headset does not shut them out completely. Practise against a timer with some background noise, speak at your normal volume, and keep talking until the time runs out.

For Indian candidates

6 habits that cost Indian candidates CELPIP marks

Your Indian accent is not scored, and Indian English is not wrong. Candidates lose marks on habits that do not fit a Canadian test built on everyday tasks, some of them learnt for other exams, and CELTESTPIP's AI feedback points many of them out in your answers.

  1. 01

    Coaching templates and memorised openers

    The habit: Openers such as In this modern era of globalisation and a template learnt word for word sound robotic, and they do not answer the task in front of you. Every test taker signs a contract to give spontaneous, original answers, and CELPIP can cancel scores for answers that are not.

    The fix: Learn a structure (your answer, two reasons, an example and a closing line) and fill it with the details of the task in front of you.

  2. 02

    Dropping word endings

    The habit: In fast speech, the -s and -ed at the end of a word are easy to swallow, and then the listener cannot tell the tense or who is doing what: Yesterday I walk to work sounds like the present.

    The fix: Finish every ending, above all when you talk about the past (Speaking Task 2): I walked, she likes, we visited. Record yourself and listen back.

  3. 03

    Speaking very softly

    The habit: With others talking in the same room, some candidates drop to a near whisper. A soft, rushed answer is harder to follow on the recording.

    The fix: Speak at your normal volume into the headset microphone, at a steady pace, as if the listener were sitting across the table.

  4. 04

    Putting on a Western accent

    The habit: Some candidates switch to an American or British accent on test day. A borrowed accent tends to slip under pressure, and that makes your speech harder to follow. Raters listen for clear, easy-to-follow speech, and CELPIP says they do not consider your accent.

    The fix: Speak in your own accent, clearly and at a steady pace. Slow down on names and numbers, and finish every sentence.

  5. 05

    Indian English phrases a Canadian reader may not know

    The habit: Prepone, revert (for reply), passed out of college and I have a doubt are everyday English in India, but a CELPIP email is written to a Canadian reader, who may not know them or may read them differently: in Canada, passed out means fainted, and a doubt means you think something may not be true. In a timed task, slips such as discuss about or a missing article (He is teacher) also creep in, and those are errors in Indian English too.

    The fix: Write move the meeting earlier, reply, graduated from college, I have a question, discuss the plan and He is a teacher.

  6. 06

    Typing with one or two fingers

    The habit: Writing is two typed tasks of 150 to 200 words each in 53 minutes. Candidates who type with one or two fingers spend the time hunting for keys and have none left to check their work.

    The fix: Practise timed typing on a keyboard before test day. The screen has a word counter and a spell-check, so spend the time you save rereading for grammar.

English tests for Canada

Which English tests does Canada accept for PR?

For permanent residence, including Express Entry, IRCC accepts three English tests: the CELPIP-General, IELTS General Training and PTE Core (accepted since 2024). IELTS Academic and PTE Academic are not accepted for PR. If you speak French, TEF Canada and TCF Canada are accepted too.

CELPIP Reading sample question

Reading, Part 1: Reading Correspondence

Hi Arjun, the building manager just emailed to say the water will be shut off on Thursday from 9 a.m. to noon for pipe repairs. I'm working from home that day, so I'll fill a few large bottles the night before. If you need some, just knock on my door. Meera

Why is Meera writing to Arjun?

  1. a)To ask him to repair the pipes
  2. b)To warn him about the water shut-off and offer help
  3. c)To invite him to work from home on Thursday
  4. d)To complain about the building manager
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: b) To warn him about the water shut-off and offer help

Meera passes on the notice about Thursday's water shut-off and offers water if Arjun needs some. Option a is the trap: the word repairs is in the message, but nobody asks Arjun to repair anything; the building manager only announced the repairs. When a question asks why someone is writing, look for the whole message, not a repeated word.

CELPIP Speaking sample task

Speaking, Task 1: Giving Advice

Your friend is moving to a new city for work and cannot decide whether to rent an apartment downtown or in the suburbs. Give your friend advice.

30 seconds to prepare, 90 seconds to speak

Tip: Keep a simple shape: give your main advice in the first sentence, back it with two reasons and an example, and close with a short summary. Practise the shape, not a script; raters notice memorised answers quickly.

What is the CELPIP exam fee in India? Is GST extra?

03

How do I pay for CELPIP in India? Can I pay by UPI?

Yes. CELPIP takes payments in India through CC Avenue, which accepts UPI, RuPay cards, net banking, wallets and EMI options from Indian banks; Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards work too, with the full amount on one card. If money left your account but the booking still shows as pending, check your CELPIP account and email info@celpip.ca with the transaction details before you pay again. Never pay anyone who offers a seat, a "leaked paper" or a score certificate on WhatsApp or social media: CELPIP treats a false score report as cheating and can report it to the government agency you apply to.
04

When are CELPIP exam dates in India, and how do I book?

When we checked on 25 September 2026, every sitting fell between Wednesday and Sunday, none on a Monday or Tuesday. Dates were listed to 24 January 2027, none was sold out, and 36 of the 37 cities had a date within two weeks. Choose your centre and date on the official CELPIP India page, pick the CELPIP-General and, for PR, Express Entry or the PGWP, the IRCC "Permanent Residence (PR)/Temporary Residence (TR)" test purpose. 05

Is there a CELPIP customer care number in India?

No. CELPIP's customer service is in Canada, and its contact page lists only Canadian numbers. 1-800-958-5186 is toll-free only within North America, so from India call +1 778-327-6854 (Vancouver) as an international call, or email info@celpip.ca, which is usually easier. The lines are open Monday to Sunday, 5:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time, which is about 6:00 p.m. to 5:30 a.m. in India (an hour later from November to mid-March), and are closed on Canadian public holidays. Test centres do not handle bookings, so contact CELPIP about a booking, payment or transfer, and never log in or pay through a link someone sends you on WhatsApp.
06

Can I give CELPIP online or at home in India?

No. The test is fully on a computer, but CELPIP's official FAQ says it must be taken in person at one of its test centres, so there is no online or at-home CELPIP in India.
07

Is the CELPIP test hard?

It depends on the level you need. CELPIP has no pass mark: each skill gets its own level, and your programme sets the minimum, such as CLB 7 in all four skills for the Federal Skilled Worker Program. The tasks are everyday Canadian situations (emails, notices, schedules and conversations), not academic texts. What catches most candidates is the clock: Reading is tight on time, each Listening recording plays only once, and other candidates in the room may be talking while you speak. Take a full mock test under test timing to see which skill needs the most work.
08

CELPIP, IELTS or PTE Core: which is easier?

None of them is officially easier: IRCC converts all three to the same CLB levels. The format is what differs. CELPIP uses a single North American accent, multiple-choice Listening and Reading, and recorded Speaking with no examiner; IELTS has a live Speaking examiner; PTE Core is a two-hour test on a computer. In India, CELPIP also costs the least (₹15,500, against ₹18,900 for PTE Core and ₹19,000 for IELTS). Try a practice test in each format and pick the one you are most comfortable with.
09

I am stuck at 6.5 in IELTS. Will CELPIP be easier?

The format changes, the standard does not. IELTS 6.5 in Reading, Writing or Speaking equals CLB 8; CLB 9 needs 7.0 (8.0 in Listening), and on CELPIP it needs a 9. Going from CLB 8 to CLB 9 adds 8 CRS points per skill for an applicant without a spouse. IELTS One Skill Retake (₹12,650) is not accepted by IRCC for PR, and a CELPIP sitting always covers all four skills, so take a full CELPIP mock exam first to see where you stand.
10

Which ID do I need for CELPIP in India? Can I use Aadhaar?

Only your passport (or a refugee travel document). Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, driving licences and voter IDs are not accepted. It must be the original, valid passport you registered with, and the name and passport number must match your registration exactly. If your passport shows only one name, email info@celpip.ca before you register to ask how to enter it. Arrive 45 minutes early: check-in closes 15 minutes before the test.
11

How long do CELPIP results take, and how long are they valid?

Scores are usually posted to your CELPIP account 2 to 4 business days after the test (CELPIP's India page says 3 to 4). For Express Entry they must be less than two years old when you create your profile and when you apply, so time your test to your application. For citizenship, IRCC accepts results even after they expire.
12

What CELPIP score do I need for Canada PR?

The Federal Skilled Worker Program needs at least CLB 7 (a CELPIP 7) in all four skills. Each level above that, up to CLB 10, adds CRS points: going from 7 to 9 in all four skills adds 56 language points for an applicant without a spouse. CLB 9 in all four skills also raises your skill transferability points when you have a post-secondary credential or foreign work experience.
13

What is the CELPIP pass mark, and does IRCC use the average score?

There is none: CELPIP is not pass or fail. Each skill gets its own level from 0 to 12, and the programme you apply to sets the minimum, for example CLB 7 in all four skills for the Federal Skilled Worker Program. IRCC does not average the levels, so 9, 9, 9 and 6 does not qualify for it.
14

Can I use CELPIP for a Canada study visa now that SDS has ended?

The Student Direct Stream ended on 8 November 2024. For admission, ask your college or university which English tests it accepts: each institution sets its own requirements. After you graduate, the Post-Graduation Work Permit accepts the CELPIP-General: CLB 7 in all four skills after any university programme, CLB 5 after a college or other non-university programme, with results less than two years old.
15

Does CELPIP have spell check, and do I need fast typing?

Yes, it has a spell-check: the official guidebook says the test screen has a timer, a word counter and a spell-check. It checks spelling only, so it will not catch a wrong word or a missing a or the. Ordinary typing is enough, but you type both Writing answers, 150 to 200 words each, in 53 minutes, so practise timed answers on a keyboard and use the word counter to stay within the limit.
16

Do I need CELPIP coaching, or can I prepare online?

Coaching is optional: CELPIP does not require it. What matters is timed practice in the real format and feedback on your Writing and Speaking. CELTESTPIP gives you both online, from anywhere in India, and if you want a person to guide you, you can find a CELPIP teacher or invite your own to follow your progress.
17

How soon can I retake CELPIP?

Plan a month between attempts: CELPIP's India page says that if you register for more than one test, every sitting must be at least 30 calendar days apart, or CELPIP can cancel the extra registration. There is no single-skill retake: each sitting covers all four skills. Results come 2 to 4 business days after the test, so check them and work on your weakest skill before you book again.
