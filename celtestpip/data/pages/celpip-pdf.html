Which CELPIP PDF should you start with?

Start with the skill that is furthest from your target. If you do not know which one that is yet, start with the score chart, find the level you need for your application, then take one free practice set in each skill and see where you fall short.

A simple order that works for most of my students:

  1. Reading and Speaking first. A Reading practice test on paper shows you how CELPIP asks questions. The Speaking templates give you a plan for all eight tasks.
  2. Then Writing. Use the Writing samples to write one email and one survey answer a day, and check each one against the checklist inside.
  3. Then Speaking with sample answers. Record your answer first, then read the sample and compare.
  4. Every day: ten minutes with the vocabulary and grammar sheets.

If you want a full plan around these, our 4-week CELPIP study plan goes week by week.

Can a PDF replace practising on the computer?

No. On test day every CELPIP section is on a computer with a timer, and Speaking is recorded into a microphone. A PDF teaches you the format and the shape of a good answer. What moves your score is answering new questions on the real clock and getting feedback.

That is what the free practice sets are for. Sign up free and you can take the first practice set of all 20 CELPIP parts and tasks. Listening and Reading sets can be repeated as often as you like, and for each Writing and Speaking task you can submit the first question for AI feedback up to 3 times. Start free CELPIP practice

CELPIP publishes its own free practice tests and sells study packs and courses on its website.
