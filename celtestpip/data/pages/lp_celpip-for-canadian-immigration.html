CELPIP for Canada PR: Which Programs Accept It (2026)

Reviewed 21 September 2026 by Matthew Zare, CELPIP Teacher.

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) accepts CELPIP-General for Express Entry, which covers the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the Canadian Experience Class and the Federal Skilled Trades Program. It is also accepted for most Provincial Nominee streams, the Post-Graduation Work Permit, the Start-up Visa and Canadian citizenship. Sponsoring a spouse needs no language test at all.

Which immigration programs accept CELPIP?

Every federal economic programme that asks for English accepts CELPIP-General. Here is the whole list in one place, with what each one asks for.

ProgrammeAccepts CELPIPMinimum
Express Entry, Federal Skilled WorkerYes, CELPIP-GeneralCLB 7 in your first official language
Express Entry, Canadian Experience ClassYes, CELPIP-GeneralCLB 7 for TEER 0 or 1 jobs, CLB 5 for TEER 2 or 3
Express Entry, Federal Skilled TradesYes, CELPIP-GeneralCLB 5 speaking and listening, CLB 4 reading and writing
Provincial Nominee ProgramsYes, in most streamsSet by the province, not by IRCC
Post-Graduation Work PermitYesCLB 7 for university, CLB 5 for college, all four skills
Start-up VisaYesCLB 5 in listening, reading, writing and speaking
Canadian citizenshipYes, General or General LSCLB 4 in speaking and listening, ages 18 to 54
Sponsoring a spouse, partner or childNo test requiredThere is no language requirement

CLB stands for Canadian Language Benchmark. It is the scale IRCC uses, and a CELPIP-General score of 7 is CLB 7, a score of 9 is CLB 9, and so on straight up the scale. That one-to-one match is the reason so many applicants pick CELPIP over the alternatives.

If you want the full conversion table and the points each level earns, that lives on our CELPIP scores and CLB requirements guide. This page is about which door your score opens.

Can I use CELPIP for Express Entry?

Yes, and IRCC is specific about which version. Its language test page says plainly: "You must take the CELPIP-General test." CELPIP-General LS, the shorter listening and speaking test, does not count for Express Entry.

Your results have to be less than two years old when you complete your profile and again when you submit your application for permanent residence. People lose their place in the pool over this. A profile that sat in the pool for twenty months with a test taken the month before is fine on day one and ineligible by the time an invitation arrives.

All of this comes from IRCC's Express Entry language test page, last updated 22 June 2026. If your scores are from IELTS or PTE Core instead, the CLB calculator converts them to CLB skill by skill.

What CELPIP score do I need for the Canadian Experience Class?

CLB 7 if your Canadian work experience is in a TEER 0 or TEER 1 job, and CLB 5 if it is TEER 2 or TEER 3. TEER is the skill category IRCC assigns to your occupation. On CELPIP-General that means a 7 or a 5 in every one of the four skills, not an average.

That last part catches people out. IRCC's Canadian Experience Class page says you must "meet the minimum score in all 4 abilities". Three nines and a six is not CLB 7. Your weakest skill sets your level, so one soft section holds back an otherwise strong result.

Those are floors, not targets. The Canadian Experience Class minimum gets you eligible; it does not get you invited. For what each level is actually worth in points, see the scores and CLB guide.

Can I use CELPIP for a Provincial Nominee Program?

Yes, in most streams, but IRCC does not set the level. The province does. IRCC's own page on how the programme works says it in one line: "Each province and territory has its own requirements."

So there is no single CLB number for PNP, and any page that hands you one is guessing. What you do instead takes five minutes. Open the stream you are applying to, on the province's own site, and read its language section. Manitoba, for example, publishes a language proficiency policy that names CELPIP-General directly. Every province has an equivalent, and the only one that matters is yours.

One thing does hold everywhere: if the stream runs through Express Entry, the federal minimum for your programme still applies on top of whatever the province asks.

Do I need CELPIP to sponsor my spouse or partner?

No. There is no language test requirement in spousal or family sponsorship, for you or for the person you are sponsoring.

This surprises a lot of people, because every other route into Canada asks for one. IRCC's sponsorship eligibility page lists what a sponsor must be: at least 18, a Canadian citizen or permanent resident or a person registered under the Indian Act, living in Canada, able to sign the undertaking and the sponsorship agreement. Language is not on the list. The page was last updated on 23 June 2026.

The same thing shows up on celpip.ca's own list of pathways, by omission. It names the Canadian Experience Class, the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the Federal Skilled Trades Program, Provincial Nominee Programs, the Post-Graduation Work Permit and the Start-up Visa. Family sponsorship is not there.

If someone has told you to book a test for a spousal application, ask them which requirement it satisfies before you pay for it.

Can I use CELPIP for a Post-Graduation Work Permit?

Yes. Since 1 November 2024, PGWP applicants have had to prove their language level, and CELPIP is one of the accepted ways to do it.

The level depends on what you studied. IRCC requires "a minimum level of Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB) 7 in English" in all four language areas for a university programme, and CLB 5 in all four for a college programme. Applications submitted before 1 November 2024 had no language requirement at all, which is why older advice on this still circulates.

Source: IRCC's PGWP eligibility page, updated 24 June 2026.

Can I use CELPIP for the Start-up Visa?

Yes. The Start-up Visa asks you to "achieve Canadian Language Benchmark 5 in listening, reading, writing and speaking", in English or French, from an approved testing agency. CELPIP-General is one of them.

CLB 5 is the lowest bar of any programme on this page. It is there because the Start-up Visa is assessed mainly on your business and your designated organisation, not on your English. Source: IRCC's Start-up Visa eligibility page, updated 21 July 2026.

Can I use CELPIP for Canadian citizenship?

Yes, and citizenship is the one place where the shorter CELPIP-General LS is enough. You need CLB 4 in speaking and listening only. There is no reading or writing requirement. This applies to applicants aged 18 to 54; outside that range you are exempt.

CLB 4 is a much lower bar than anything Express Entry asks for, so if you already have a valid CELPIP-General result from a PR application, it almost certainly clears it.

The score side of citizenship has its own page: what CELPIP score you need for Canadian citizenship.

Which CELPIP test do I take, CELPIP-General or CELPIP-General LS?

Take CELPIP-General unless your only goal is citizenship. General covers all four skills and is the version IRCC names for Express Entry. General LS covers listening and speaking only, which is exactly what citizenship asks for and nothing like enough for permanent residence.

The naming gets confusing fast, including the "CELPIP-G" label you will see on IRCC's own forms and tables. Our CELPIP test types guide covers all of it properly, and it is the page to read before you book.

If I have taken CELPIP more than once, which result do I submit?

Start with what is actually published, because there is less of it than you would expect.

On the CELPIP side, celpip.ca is clear. Your scores appear in your CELPIP Account in 2 to 4 business days after your test date, and they stay there for two years from that date. Nothing is sent to IRCC for you. You download the Official Score Report as a PDF from your account and submit it yourself. You can also only register for one test session in any five calendar days.

On the IRCC side, the published rules are that your results must be less than two years old both when you complete your Express Entry profile and when you apply, that you update your profile if you take a new test, and that expired results make a profile ineligible.

IRCC does not publish a rule for choosing between two results that are both still valid. We have looked, on the Express Entry language test page, the language testing page, the create-your-profile page and the help centre. None of them addresses it.

So we are not going to invent an answer for you, and you should be careful with any prep site that does. This is a question for IRCC directly or for a licensed immigration consultant or lawyer, who are the only people allowed to advise you on it.

How long is my CELPIP result valid?

Two years, and the date that matters is your test date, not the date you got your result.

Two separate clocks run on the same two years and it helps to see them side by side.

ClockWhat it governs
IRCCResults must be under two years old when you complete your profile and when you apply
Your CELPIP accountScores stay viewable and downloadable for two years from your test date

Plan backwards from the second one. If your account access lapses, getting hold of the report again is an errand you do not want in the middle of an application.

Can I use CELPIP for an Australian visa?

Yes, with two conditions. Australia's Department of Home Affairs accepts CELPIP General for tests taken at a secure test centre on or after 7 August 2025, and it does not accept CELPIP Online.

This is recent, and a lot of prep material has not caught up with it. If Australia is your plan, check the test centre and the delivery format before you book, not after.

How many CRS points will my CELPIP score earn?

Language is the single largest thing you control in the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS), the score IRCC ranks Express Entry candidates on. Moving from CLB 7 to CLB 9 across all four skills is worth more than most applicants realise, and it stacks again through the skill transferability factors.

The full points tables, single and with a spouse, live on the CELPIP scores and CLB requirements guide. We keep them in one place rather than repeating them here, so there is only ever one version to keep current.

If you are weighing CELPIP against the alternative, our CELPIP vs IELTS comparison covers which one suits which candidate.

To see your full score, not just the language part, use the Express Entry CRS calculator. It follows IRCC's criteria line by line, single or with a spouse, and shows which change would add the most points.

Frequently asked questions

Is CELPIP accepted by IRCC?

+
Yes. CELPIP-General is a designated test for Canadian permanent residence, and CELPIP-General LS is designated for citizenship. Both are recognised by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada.

Can I use CELPIP-General LS for permanent residence?

+
No. Express Entry requires CELPIP-General. General LS covers listening and speaking only and is for citizenship.

Does CELPIP work for every Provincial Nominee Program?

+
It is accepted in most streams, but each province sets its own requirements, so check the stream you are applying to on the province's own site.

Do I need a language test to sponsor my spouse?

+
No. Spousal and family sponsorship carries no language requirement for the sponsor or the person being sponsored.

How fast do CELPIP results come back?

+
Scores appear in your CELPIP Account in 2 to 4 business days after your test date, according to celpip.ca.

How soon can I take CELPIP again?

+
You can register for no more than one test session in any five calendar days.
