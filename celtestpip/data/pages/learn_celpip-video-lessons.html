CELPIP Video Lessons FAQ

Quick answers to the questions test takers ask us most.

Still have a question?Ask the AI CELPIP Coach, any time.
01

Are the CELPIP video lessons free?

Yes. Every video on this page is public on YouTube and free to watch. The written lessons they belong to are the free courses on CELTESTPIP; practice sets beyond the free questions are part of Pro.

02

Do I need to watch the videos in order?

Inside one course, yes. The Writing course starts with orientation and scoring and ends with timing, and the Speaking course puts the score video before Task 1, because each lesson assumes the one before it. Across courses, start with the section your practice scores say is weakest.

03

Where are the new videos announced?

On the channel. Subscribe to CELTESTPIP on YouTube and new lessons land in your subscriptions the day they publish; this page is updated at the same time.

04

Is this the official CELPIP YouTube channel?

No. The official channel is CELPIP Test, run by the makers of the test. These videos come from CELTESTPIP, an independent CELPIP preparation site, and teach each task with our own examples and sample answers. Subscribe to CELTESTPIP on YouTube to get each new lesson the day it publishes.

Still have a question?Ask the AI CELPIP Coach, any time.

Every video lesson on the CELTESTPIP YouTube channel is on this page, grouped by course and in the order the course teaches it. Writing and Speaking each have a full course on video, Reading lessons are publishing part by part, and Listening has practice episodes. Each video links to the written lesson it belongs to and to its full playlist.

How to use these videos

Watch them in order inside one course, not one from each. The Writing course runs from orientation and scoring to timing, and the Speaking course runs Task 1 to Task 8 with the score video first, so a video assumes you have seen the ones above it. Most are 9 to 17 minutes long. Watch one, open the lesson page under it, and do the practice set before the next one. That is how the courses are built, and it is how the students who finish them use them.

Every course lesson page on the site embeds its own video too, so you can also watch as you read. New lessons publish on the channel first: subscribe to CELTESTPIP on YouTube so they reach you the day they go live.
