Why We Built CELTESTPIP

Preparing for the CELPIP exam used to mean spending money on tutors, waiting days for feedback on your Writing and Speaking, and finding too few full-length tests to practice on. We wanted to change that.

CELTESTPIP was founded in Canada in 2024 by a team of language-learning technology specialists and test preparation experts. We set out to build the most complete CELPIP practice platform available online, one where you could take realistic mock exams, get scored instantly by AI, and actually understand your mistakes with detailed explanations.

Today, over 170,000+ test takers worldwide use CELTESTPIP to prepare for their CELPIP exam. Learners on our platform have answered more than 22.5M+ questions and received over 3.6M+ AI-generated scores. Whether you are applying for Canadian permanent residency through Express Entry, Canadian citizenship, professional licensing, or an Australian visa (DHA-accepted), CELTESTPIP gives you the practice and feedback you need to walk into test day feeling ready.
