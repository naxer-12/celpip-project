Listening Part 2 of the CELPIP Test evaluates how well you can understand casual, everyday conversations. The conversations reflect typical social interactions between people and are followed by questions that test your ability to recall facts, interpret meaning, and draw conclusions.

🧩 Overview of Listening Part 2

  • Focus: Understanding daily life conversations (e.g., family discussions, co-worker chats).
  • Speakers: Always two people: one man and one woman.
  • Length: One audio clip, approximately 1.5 to 2 minutes long.
  • Playback: Audio plays once only, with no replay.
  • Questions: Around 5 questions, read aloud one at a time.
  • No question text shown, so you must rely on listening.

🎧 What You'll Hear

In this part, the conversation may include:

  • Family members discussing plans
  • Friends talking about problems
  • Co-workers handling tasks
  • People making everyday decisions

These are natural, real-life scenarios where the tone may be casual and emotionally expressive.

🧠 Types of Questions

You may be asked:

1. General Meaning

  • Asks about the overall idea or purpose.
  • Requires understanding the big picture.

2. Specific Information

  • Focuses on a detail mentioned in the conversation.
  • Could involve numbers, names, reasons, etc.

3. Inference

  • Requires reading between the lines.
  • You must use clues from the conversation to figure out what’s implied.

📝 Note-Taking Tips

Because you only hear the audio once and won’t see the questions in writing, note-taking is crucial.

Tips for Effective Notes:

  • Use a T-chart: One column for each speaker.
  • Capture the 5Ws: Who, What, Where, When, Why.
  • Focus on main ideas and any problems or plans.
  • Use abbreviations and symbols (e.g., $, →, ?, J for Jason).
  • Write quickly. Don’t worry about spelling or grammar.
  • Only you will see your notes; they’re not graded.

Example structure:

Notepad
| Man         | Woman        |
|-------------|--------------|
| traffic bad | dinner not ready |
| J failed    | consoling J  |
| J nervous   | J needs practice |
| suggested bike | wants to drive (snowboard) |

🎯 Answering Strategies

During the Test:

  • Listen actively, both to the conversation and the questions.
  • After the audio, listen carefully to each spoken question.
  • Match question content with your notes to choose the best answer.
  • Use process of elimination to narrow choices.
  • Guess if unsure. There’s no penalty for wrong answers.

Tip:

Questions typically follow the chronological order of the conversation. Use your notes from top to bottom to locate answers.

💬 Common Topics in Part 2

  • Transportation (e.g., learning to drive)
  • Weekend or vacation plans
  • Family challenges or decisions
  • Workplace planning
  • Local events or errands

🛎 Final Reminders

  • You only hear the audio and questions once, so focus is essential.
  • Use the 30-second response window to think and answer.
  • Guess instead of skipping. Unanswered questions score 0 points.
  • The earlier parts (like Part 2) are usually easier, so maximize your score here.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Listening Part 2.

What topics are in CELPIP Listening Part 2?

CELPIP Listening Part 2 (Daily Life Conversation) is a casual everyday conversation: family members discussing plans, friends talking about a problem, co-workers handling a task, or people deciding something like learning to drive. The tone is natural and sometimes emotional.

How long is the audio in CELPIP Listening Part 2 and how many questions?

CELPIP Listening Part 2 is one conversation of about 1.5–2 minutes, played once, followed by around 5 questions read aloud one at a time. The questions are not shown as text, so you rely entirely on listening and your notes.

Do CELPIP Listening Part 2 questions follow the order of the conversation?

Yes. CELPIP Listening Part 2 questions usually appear in the same chronological order as the conversation. That means you can read your two-column speaker notes from top to bottom to locate each answer as it comes up.

Should I guess on CELPIP Listening Part 2?

Always guess on CELPIP Listening Part 2 rather than leave a question blank. An unanswered question scores zero and there is no penalty for a wrong answer. Since Part 2 is one of the easier sections, secure as many marks as you can.

Can I replay the audio in CELPIP Listening Part 2?

No. In CELPIP Listening Part 2 every clip plays once, with no replay or pause. That is why note-taking is essential: capture names, plans, and any problem as you listen, because you cannot go back to check a detail.

What kind of questions are asked in CELPIP Listening Part 2?

CELPIP Listening Part 2 asks about the main idea, specific details, and the speakers' feelings or intentions. Some questions test a fact from the conversation, while others ask what a speaker meant or how they felt, so note tone as well as content.

How do I improve my CELPIP Listening Part 2 score?

Improving at CELPIP Listening Part 2 comes down to following natural, emotional conversations and building a fast two-column note habit. Practice catching both detail and intention, since this everyday-conversation section tests the two together.
