CELPIP Vocabulary FAQ

Quick answers to the questions test takers ask us most.

01

How many words should I learn for CELPIP?

For a strong score (CLB 7+), aim to master 80–100 key vocabulary words per task type. Our bundles are curated by our teachers around the everyday situations each CELPIP task type covers.

02

What makes these word bundles different from a dictionary?

Each word is selected specifically for CELPIP relevance and includes exam-focused tips — such as when to use formal vs. informal language, or which synonyms score higher in speaking responses.

03

Can I track my vocabulary progress?

Yes. When you open a word, it is automatically saved to your personal word bank. You can mark words as mastered, add personal notes, and see how many words you have learned in each bundle.

Why Vocabulary Matters for CELPIP

A strong vocabulary is one of the most effective ways to improve your CELPIP score across all four skills. Each word bundle on this page is carefully curated for a specific CELPIP exam task — from Speaking Task 1 through to Listening Part 6 — so you study words chosen for the everyday situations each task covers.

Every word includes a clear definition, an example sentence in context, phonetic pronunciation, and a CELPIP-specific tip explaining how and when to use the word during the test. Words are organized by difficulty and relevance, making it easy to start with the most impactful vocabulary first.
