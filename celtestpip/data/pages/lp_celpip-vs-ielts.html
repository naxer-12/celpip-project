CELPIP vs IELTS 2026: Which English Test Should You Take?

Reviewed November 2026 by Matthew Zare, CELPIP Teacher

CELPIP-General costs $299 CAD plus tax. IELTS General Training runs about $361 plus tax. CELPIP results usually arrive in a few business days, IELTS in 3 to 13. Both are accepted by IRCC and both convert to the same CLB scale, but IELTS converts per skill: CLB 9 needs IELTS Listening 8.0 while the other three skills need only 7.0.

The fastest way to settle this is to sit one full CELPIP under real timing and see how the format feels. No card required.

What is the difference between CELPIP and IELTS?

CELPIP is fully computer-based and built around Canadian English. IELTS gives you a paper or computer option and puts you in a room with a human examiner for Speaking. That single difference, a microphone versus a person, is what most test takers actually end up choosing on.

CELPIP

CELPIP (Canadian English Language Proficiency Index Program) is developed by Paragon Testing Enterprises, a Prometric company. IRCC (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada) accepts it for permanent residence, and Australia's Department of Home Affairs accepts it for visa applications.

There are two versions: CELPIP-General, which covers all four skills and is the one IRCC takes for permanent residence, and CELPIP-General LS, which covers Listening and Speaking only and is the one IRCC takes for citizenship.

IELTS

IELTS (International English Language Testing System) is run by the British Council, IDP and Cambridge. It also has two versions, Academic and General Training.

For Canadian immigration only General Training counts. Academic will not be accepted, no matter how strong the score.

Both tests measure the same four skills, and both get translated into CLB (Canadian Language Benchmark) levels before IRCC looks at them. CLB is the scale that actually decides your file. The test is just how you get there.

Is CELPIP 9 the same as IELTS 7?

No. A CELPIP 9 across all four skills equals CLB 9 across all four skills, and IELTS 7.0 across all four does not. IELTS 7.0 gives you CLB 9 in Reading, Writing and Speaking, but only CLB 7 in Listening. To reach CLB 9 in Listening you need an IELTS 8.0.

This is why the Express Entry target you see written as 8777 exists. In the usual Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking order, that is Listening 8.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 7.0, Speaking 7.0, and it is the minimum IELTS combination that gives CLB 9 straight across. On CELPIP the equivalent is a flat 9, 9, 9, 9. The same trap appears one level down, and one level below that it disappears:

Why does CLB 9 need IELTS Listening 8.0 when the other skills need 7.0?

Because IRCC sets the CLB thresholds skill by skill, and the IELTS Listening band scale sits differently against the CLB descriptors than the other three do. It is a feature of the conversion chart, not a mistake, and IRCC has published it this way for years.

The practical effect is worth spelling out. IELTS Listening runs 6.0 at CLB 7, then jumps to 7.5 for CLB 8 and 8.0 for CLB 9. There is no half step. Reading, Writing and Speaking all move in smaller, more even increments.

Any site that tells you IELTS 7.0 simply equals CELPIP 9, or prints a single-column conversion where one IELTS band maps to one CELPIP number, has flattened four different scales into one. For a general sense of your English that is harmless. For an Express Entry profile it can cost you an invitation, because the profile is scored on your lowest ability, not your average.

CELPIP avoids the problem by mapping one to one. A CELPIP 9 is a CLB 9 in every skill, every time, with no chart to consult.

What is your CELPIP score in IELTS, skill by skill?

Your CELPIP level in one skill converts to a different IELTS band in each of the four skills, so there is no single answer. CELPIP 8 is IELTS 6.5 in Reading, Writing and Speaking, but IELTS 7.5 in Listening. Find your skill below and take the number that applies to it, not an average.

What is CELPIP Writing 8 in IELTS?

CELPIP Writing 8 is IELTS Writing 6.5. A CELPIP 8 is a CLB 8, and IRCC's chart puts CLB 8 Writing at IELTS 6.5. One level up, CELPIP Writing 9 is IELTS Writing 7.0.

Writing is the skill where the two tests are closest, and it is also the one people most often assume they need to retake. If your file needs CLB 9 and your CELPIP Writing came back at 8, you are half a band short in IELTS terms, which on CELPIP means one level. Before you book anything, get a scored response back on a real Task 1 email and find out whether the gap is structure or vocabulary, because those two take very different amounts of time to fix. What is CELPIP Reading 8 in IELTS? And CELPIP Reading 7?

CELPIP Reading 8 is IELTS Reading 6.5, and CELPIP Reading 7 is IELTS Reading 6.0. Reading is the skill where the top of the chart moves fastest: CELPIP Reading 10 is IELTS Reading 8.0, a full band above what CLB 10 asks of Writing.

That top-end gap matters if you are chasing CLB 10 for maximum CRS (Comprehensive Ranking System) points. On IELTS you would need an 8.0 in Reading, and on CELPIP you need a 10, which is the same number of correct answers away from a 9 as any other step on the scale. For what each CELPIP Reading level requires in raw terms, see how CELPIP Reading scores convert to CLB.

What is CELPIP Speaking 8 in IELTS?

CELPIP Speaking 8 is IELTS Speaking 6.5. CELPIP Speaking 9 is IELTS Speaking 7.0, and CELPIP Speaking 10 is IELTS Speaking 7.5.

The conversion is straightforward. What is not straightforward is that you earn these two scores in completely different rooms. CELPIP Speaking is 15 minutes of recording your answers to a microphone across 8 tasks, with a countdown running. IELTS Speaking is a face-to-face interview with an examiner, often on a separate day from the rest of your test. People who freeze in front of a stranger and people who freeze in front of a timer are rarely the same people, so the band you can reach depends partly on which of those you are.

What is CELPIP Listening 9 in IELTS?

CELPIP Listening 9 is IELTS Listening 8.0. This is the single widest gap between the two tests, and it is the number that costs people invitations.

Look at what happens between CLB 7 and CLB 8 in Listening. IELTS goes from 6.0 to 7.5, a jump of one and a half bands. In Reading, Writing and Speaking the same step is half a band, from 6.0 to 6.5. Listening moves three times as far as any other skill at exactly the point where most Express Entry files are decided. At the top of the chart it is the same story: CLB 10 Listening needs IELTS 8.5, the highest number IRCC publishes anywhere in the equivalency table.

CELPIP has no equivalent step, because a CELPIP Listening 9 is a CLB 9 and that is the end of it. If Listening is the skill standing between you and CLB 9, practise CELPIP Listening free and see how the format compares before you commit to another IELTS sitting.

How much does CELPIP cost compared to IELTS in 2026?

CELPIP-General is $299 CAD plus tax. IELTS General Training is about $361 CAD plus tax. That is roughly a $62 gap before tax on a single sitting, and it grows if you end up taking either test twice.

$299 is a fee, not a total. The same $299 appears in Ontario at 13% HST and in Alberta at 5%, so what you actually pay at checkout depends on your province. Our full breakdown covers what CELPIP costs in 2026 including tax by province, rescheduling and refunds.

How fast do CELPIP and IELTS results come back?

CELPIP results are usually online in a few business days. IELTS takes 3 to 5 days for the computer version and up to 13 days on paper. If you are working to a draw date or a document deadline, that difference is the whole argument.

Result timeFormat
CELPIP-GeneralUsually a few business daysOnline in your CELPIP Account, PDF download
IELTS, computer-delivered3 to 5 daysTest Report Form
IELTS, paper-basedup to 13 daysTest Report Form

Business days means weekends and holidays do not count, so a Friday CELPIP sitting can land the following Wednesday or Thursday. One detail that catches people out: CELPIP no longer issues hard-copy score reports. Everything is online. When IRCC asks for your results, you sign in to your CELPIP Account and download the official PDF yourself. Your scores stay viewable there for two years from your test date.

If you got a score you want a second opinion on, CELPIP lets you request a re-evaluation within six months of the test date, and the result comes back in roughly one to two weeks. If your level changes, Paragon refunds the re-evaluation fee for that component. Worth knowing: Paragon says re-evaluating Listening and Reading is unlikely to change anything, because those two are computer rated. Save the request for Writing or Speaking.

How long are CELPIP and IELTS scores valid?

Both are valid for two years, and IRCC checks that twice. Your results must be less than two years old when you complete your Express Entry profile and when you submit your application for permanent residence.

That second check is the one people miss. A score that was fresh when you entered the pool can expire while you wait for an invitation, and then it is not just your profile at risk, it is the application itself. If your test is approaching 18 months old and you are still in the pool, start planning a retake rather than hoping for a draw.

Which tests does IRCC accept for Express Entry?

Three English tests: CELPIP-General, IELTS General Training, and PTE Core. Each has its own official conversion chart, and the version you take matters as much as the test you choose.

CELPIPCELPIP-GeneralCELPIP-General LS (that one is for citizenship)
IELTSGeneral TrainingAcademic, and IELTS One Skill Retake

IRCC does not accept IELTS One Skill Retake for Express Entry. This is worth reading twice, because One Skill Retake is marketed hard as a way to fix a single weak band, and for a Canadian PR file it does not work. IRCC states it plainly on its own language test page. If your Listening came in at 7.0 and you need 8.0, you have to sit the whole IELTS again.

CELPIP has no equivalent product, so the same situation means retaking the full test either way. The difference is that CELPIP's full test is one sitting of under three hours and $299, while a full IELTS retake is about $361 and a separate Speaking appointment.

If you are weighing a third option, PTE Core is on IRCC's accepted list with its own published CLB chart. It is outside the scope of this comparison, but do not assume the choice is only between two tests. How many CRS points does each CLB level give you?

Language is the single biggest lever in your CRS (Comprehensive Ranking System) score, and the points come from your CLB level, not from which test you took. A CLB 9 earned on CELPIP and a CLB 9 earned on IELTS are worth exactly the same. Points below are per ability, so multiply by four for your first official language.

Run the arithmetic on your own file before you book anything. Moving from CLB 7 to CLB 9 in all four skills, without a spouse, is (31 minus 17) times 4, which is 56 CRS points. There is no other single change in your profile that moves your score that far that fast. This is also the honest argument against aiming at the CLB 7 minimum. CLB 7 gets you eligible. CLB 9 gets you invited.

CELPIP Listening vs IELTS Listening: what actually differs?

CELPIP Listening runs 46 to 55 minutes across 6 parts and 38 questions. IELTS Listening runs about 30 minutes across 4 parts and 40 questions, plus 10 minutes to copy your answers onto an answer sheet on the paper test. CELPIP is the longer sitting; IELTS asks you to write your answers down.

CELPIP-General ListeningIELTS General Training Listening
Time46 to 55 minutesabout 30 minutes, plus 10 minutes to transfer answers on paper
Parts64
Questions3840
What you listen toProblem solving, a daily-life conversation, information, a news item, a discussion, viewpointsTwo everyday or social recordings, then two in an educational or training context
AccentsEveryday English and vocabulary from everyday situationsBritish, Australian, New Zealand and North American
How you answerOn screen, from answer choicesWritten onto an answer sheet
SpellingNot part of what is ratedMarks are lost for incorrect spelling
RatingComputer ratedMarked against the answer key

Two of those rows decide most people's score. The first is accents: IELTS deliberately mixes four accent groups in one 30-minute test, so you can lose a section to an unfamiliar voice rather than to weak listening. Paragon does not publish an accent list for CELPIP and describes the test instead as everyday English and vocabulary taken from everyday situations, which is a narrower target to prepare for.

The second is spelling. On IELTS you write your answers, and a word spelled wrong is simply wrong, even when you heard it perfectly. CELPIP Listening has you choose from answer options on screen and is computer rated, so a shaky speller loses nothing to spelling in this section.

The trade is time. CELPIP asks for up to 55 minutes of sustained attention against IELTS's 30, and concentration fatigue is real in the sixth part of anything. That is a stamina problem, and stamina is the one thing you can fix by sitting full-length mocks rather than doing exercises. CELPIP Reading vs IELTS Reading: what actually differs?

CELPIP Reading runs 43 to 56 minutes across 4 parts and 38 questions. IELTS General Training Reading runs 60 minutes across 3 sections and 40 questions, with no extra transfer time. The bigger difference is not length, it is that IELTS uses eleven different question types and CELPIP uses far fewer.

CELPIP-General ReadingIELTS General Training Reading
Time43 to 56 minutes60 minutes, transfer time included
Sections43
Questions3840
What you readCorrespondence, a diagram to apply, information, viewpointsEveryday texts, then work-related texts, then one long general-interest text
Question typesAnswer choices on screenEleven, including True/False/Not Given, matching headings, sentence completion and short answer
SpellingNot part of what is ratedShort-answer and completion questions are marked on the word you write
RatingComputer ratedMarked against the answer key

The eleven question types are the real workload. True/False/Not Given alone defeats people who read English perfectly well, because it tests a rule about evidence rather than comprehension, and Not Given has to be learned as a category. Matching headings and sentence completion each carry their own technique. That is test strategy you have to study on top of your English, and it is the honest reason some people find IELTS Reading harder than their actual reading level.

CELPIP Reading gives you answer choices on screen and is computer rated, so what you are practising is reading, not question formats. It also gives you a diagram-based part that IELTS has no equivalent of, which catches people who skim. Is CELPIP easier than IELTS?

Neither test is officially easier. Both are built to measure the same level of English, and both are accepted at the same CLB thresholds. What changes is which one suits how you work.

We are not going to pretend otherwise, because you will find a hundred forum threads saying CELPIP is easier and a hundred saying the opposite, and both groups are describing their own strengths rather than the tests. Here is the honest split.

CELPIP tends to suit you if...

  • You type comfortably
  • You are already living in Canada and recognise the scenarios
  • You would rather talk to a microphone than a stranger
  • Spelling is a weak point (Listening and Reading answers are chosen on screen, not written)
  • You have three weeks: fewer question types means less test strategy to learn

IELTS tends to suit you if...

  • You learned British English at school
  • You have already been preparing with IELTS materials
  • You read questions before listening and want that option back
  • You perform better in conversation with a person than into a recording

One thing that genuinely is easier on CELPIP: the conversion. Your score is your CLB level. No chart, no per-skill lookup, no discovering after results day that your Listening band was half a point short of what you assumed. The only reliable way to answer this for yourself is to sit both formats. Try free CELPIP practice questions and see which one your brain prefers before you spend $299.

Are CELPIP and IELTS the same test?

No. They are separate tests from separate organisations, with different formats, different scoring scales and different fees. What they share is the destination: both convert to CLB, and IRCC treats the resulting CLB level identically.

You cannot mix them. One test, all four skills, one score report. There is no combining a CELPIP Speaking result with an IELTS Reading result, and neither test lets you retake a single section for Canadian immigration purposes.

Can you take CELPIP outside Canada?

Yes. CELPIP runs test centres in around 40 countries, including India, the Philippines, the United Arab Emirates, Nigeria, the United Kingdom, the United States, Australia, Pakistan, Brazil and China.

Pricing outside Canada is set in local currency. Recent examples from the live booking list: US$255 in Alameda, California; ₹15,500 at every centre in India; ₱10,000 at every centre in the Philippines; ₦229,000 at every centre in Nigeria (centres, dates and how to pay are in our guides to CELPIP in India, the Philippines and Nigeria). Do not convert the $299 Canadian figure and assume that is your price, and check availability early, because non-Canadian centres run fewer dates.

CELPIP scores for Australian visas (DHA)

If you are applying to Australia rather than Canada, CELPIP is accepted by the Department of Home Affairs, and Paragon publishes the level each DHA band needs:

When should you choose IELTS instead?

Take IELTS if your destination is not Canada or Australia, or if you need the Academic module for a university application. Those are the two cases where CELPIP genuinely cannot help you.

IELTS is also the right call if you have been studying with IELTS materials for months and your test date is close. Switching format three weeks out to save $62 is a bad trade. Familiarity is worth more than the price gap when the deadline is real.

And if you want a paper test, IELTS is your only option. CELPIP is computer-delivered everywhere.

How do you prepare for CELPIP and get a high score?

Start with a full mock exam, not with study material. You cannot plan around a weakness you have not measured, and most people are wrong about which of their four skills is actually lowest.

1

Sit one full mock under real timing

No pausing, no breaks. This gives you a baseline CLB level and shows you which skill is holding your file back. The mock exam link at the top of this page is the place to start.

2

Put your hours where the gap is

If Speaking and Writing are lowest, that is where the fastest gains live, because both respond quickly to feedback in a way Reading rarely does.

3

Get scored feedback daily on Writing and Speaking

Submitting a response and reading a detailed breakdown at each CLB level teaches you the difference between a CLB 8 answer and a CLB 9 answer far faster than reading advice about it.

4

Rebuild your stamina before test day

Two or three more full mocks under strict conditions. Under three hours is not long in theory and very long in practice.

Practise by section to target the gap. If your weak skill is Writing, our CELPIP Writing score chart shows what each level actually requires. For Listening, the CELPIP Listening score chart does the same, and Listening is the skill most likely to be the one standing between you and CLB 9.

Which test should you choose?

If Canada is your destination, take CELPIP. It is $62 cheaper, it finishes in one sitting, results usually come back in a few business days, and your score is your CLB level with no conversion chart to get wrong.

The conversion point is the one we would push hardest, and it is not a marketing line. Every year we see people who took IELTS, scored 7.0 across the board, assumed that was CLB 9, and found out at profile time that their Listening was CLB 7. On CELPIP that mistake is not available to make.

Take IELTS if you are applying outside Canada and Australia, if you need Academic for a university, or if you are already deep into IELTS preparation with a close test date.

And if you are still genuinely unsure, stop reading comparisons and go sit a format. An hour with a real CELPIP mock will tell you more than any table on this page.

CELPIP vs IELTS: Frequently Asked Questions

Is CELPIP easier than IELTS?+
Neither is officially easier. Both measure the same level of English against the same CLB thresholds. CELPIP suits people who type comfortably, prefer Canadian accents, and would rather record their Speaking than sit with an examiner. IELTS suits people already familiar with British English or already prepared with IELTS materials.
What is CELPIP 9 in IELTS?+
CELPIP 9 equals CLB 9. On IELTS General Training that means Reading 7.0, Writing 7.0, Listening 8.0 and Speaking 7.0. Listening is the outlier, and an IELTS 7.0 in Listening only reaches CLB 7.
What is IELTS 6.5 in CELPIP?+
It depends on the skill. IELTS 6.5 gives CLB 8 in Reading, Writing and Speaking, which is CELPIP 8, but only CLB 7 in Listening, which is CELPIP 7.
What is CELPIP 10 in IELTS?+
CELPIP 10 equals CLB 10, which on IELTS is Reading 8.0, Writing 7.5, Listening 8.5 and Speaking 7.5.
Does IRCC accept IELTS One Skill Retake?+
No. IRCC does not accept IELTS One Skill Retake for Express Entry. If one band is short you must sit the full test again.
Which test gives more CRS points?+
Neither. CRS points come from your CLB level, not from the test you took. A CLB 9 is worth the same either way.
How long are CELPIP and IELTS scores valid?+
Two years for both. IRCC requires your results to be under two years old when you create your Express Entry profile and again when you submit your application for permanent residence.
How much cheaper is CELPIP than IELTS?+
CELPIP-General is $299 plus tax and IELTS General Training is about $361 plus tax in Canada, a gap of roughly $62 before tax.
How fast do CELPIP results come back?+
Usually a few business days, online in your CELPIP Account. Hard-copy score reports have been discontinued.
Can I take CELPIP outside Canada?+
Yes, in roughly 40 countries, priced in local currency rather than Canadian dollars.
Do Canadian universities accept CELPIP?+
CELPIP-General is accepted by a range of universities, colleges and vocational programs, alongside its IRCC and Australian DHA recognition. Check your specific institution, because acceptance is set program by program.
Can I retake just one section of CELPIP or IELTS?+
Not for Canadian immigration. CELPIP has no single-section retake, and IRCC does not accept IELTS One Skill Retake for Express Entry.
What is CELPIP Writing 8 in IELTS?+
CELPIP Writing 8 is IELTS Writing 6.5. A CELPIP 8 equals CLB 8, and IRCC puts CLB 8 Writing at IELTS 6.5. CELPIP Writing 9 is IELTS Writing 7.0.
What is CELPIP Reading 8 in IELTS?+
CELPIP Reading 8 is IELTS Reading 6.5. CELPIP Reading 7 is IELTS Reading 6.0, and CELPIP Reading 10 is IELTS Reading 8.0, the highest Reading band IRCC's chart lists.
What is CELPIP Reading 7 in IELTS?+
CELPIP Reading 7 is IELTS Reading 6.0. CLB 7 is the only level where all four skills convert to the same IELTS band, 6.0 across the board.
What is CELPIP Speaking 8 in IELTS?+
CELPIP Speaking 8 is IELTS Speaking 6.5. CELPIP Speaking 9 is IELTS Speaking 7.0. The scores convert cleanly, but CELPIP Speaking is 15 minutes recorded to a microphone while IELTS Speaking is a face-to-face interview.
What is CELPIP Listening 9 in IELTS?+
CELPIP Listening 9 is IELTS Listening 8.0. Listening is the widest gap between the two tests. To reach CLB 8 in Listening, IELTS asks for 7.5 against 6.0 for CLB 7, a jump of one and a half bands where every other skill moves half a band.
Is CELPIP Listening easier than IELTS Listening?+
Neither is officially easier, but they test differently. CELPIP Listening runs 46 to 55 minutes over 6 parts with answer choices on screen and is computer rated, so spelling costs you nothing. IELTS Listening runs about 30 minutes over 4 parts, mixes British, Australian, New Zealand and North American accents, and takes marks off for spelling because you write your answers down.
