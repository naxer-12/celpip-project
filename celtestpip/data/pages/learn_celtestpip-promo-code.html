CELTESTPIP Promo Code: How to Get 15% Off Pro (2026)

There is no CELTESTPIP promo code. Get 15% off Pro with a friend's or teacher's invite link, applied at checkout. And how to earn $10 for each invite.

There is no CELTESTPIP promo code, and you do not need one. The only discount is 15% off your first CELTESTPIP Pro purchase, and it comes through an invite link from a friend, classmate or teacher who already uses CELTESTPIP. Open their link, create your free account from that page, and the 15% comes off at checkout by itself. There is nothing to type.

If a coupon site lists a "CELTESTPIP promo code", it did not come from us. We do not publish codes, so those pages cannot give you one that works.

Is there a CELTESTPIP promo code or discount code?

No. CELTESTPIP does not use promo codes, coupon codes or discount codes. The one discount we offer is the 15% invite discount on Pro, and it is applied automatically when you sign up through an invite link. That keeps it simple: you never have to hunt for a code at checkout, and nobody can sell you a fake one.

How do I get 15% off CELTESTPIP Pro?

Ask someone who uses CELTESTPIP for their invite link. Every CELTESTPIP account has its own link, so a friend, a classmate or your CELPIP teacher can send you theirs in a minute. Then:

  1. Open the invite link. You land on a page that says you have unlocked 15% off.
  2. Create your free CELTESTPIP account from that page.
  3. Choose Pro when you are ready. The 15% is applied at checkout automatically, with no code. It applies to your first Pro purchase only, so later renewals are at the normal price.

If you study with a CELPIP teacher, ask them first. Teachers can join our affiliate program and share one link with all their students.

What can I use free before I pay?

More than most people expect. With a free account you can take the first practice set in every one of the 20 CELPIP parts and tasks, on the real clock, with the answer key. Your first Writing and Speaking answers get AI feedback up to three times per task, and every lesson in the free CELTESTPIP courses is free. Start with the free practice sets to find your weakest skill, then decide whether Pro is worth it for you.

Pro unlocks the rest: every other practice question with feedback and the full timed mock exams. If you are still comparing options, our page to compare CELPIP practice platforms puts the main ones side by side, and you can read CELTESTPIP reviews from learners first.

How do I earn $10 by inviting others?

Share your own invite link. You earn $10 for every purchase made through it, and the person you invite gets 15% off. Your link is ready as soon as you create a free account, in Invite & Earn inside your account. A dashboard shows your clicks, checkouts and earnings, and you can withdraw to your bank or PayPal.

If you are a teacher, an immigration consultant, a school or a content creator, the CELTESTPIP affiliate program explains how it works for you.

Are you a CELPIP teacher?

Join our teacher directory free, then share your invite link with your students. Create your CELPIP teacher profile and, once we approve it (usually within a day), it appears in our CELPIP teacher directory, where learners who practise on CELTESTPIP send class requests. There is no joining fee and no commission: you set your own rate and keep everything you charge. When a student connects with you, you can see their mock exam scores, speaking and writing submissions and practice history, so your feedback is based on their real results.

If you already have students, send them your invite link. They get 15% off their first Pro purchase, and you earn $10 for each purchase.

Is there a discount code for the CELPIP test itself?

Not from us. CELTESTPIP is an independent practice platform. We are not part of Paragon Testing Enterprises, the company behind CELPIP, and we cannot give any discount on the CELPIP test fee. Our 15% applies to CELTESTPIP Pro only. You pay the test fee when you book on CELPIP's own website, and our CELPIP cost guide explains the fee and tax by province.
