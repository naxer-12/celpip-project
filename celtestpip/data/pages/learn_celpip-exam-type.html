CELPIP General vs LS: Which Test Do You Need in 2026?

CELPIP General tests all four skills: the test for PR, work permits and Australia. CELPIP General LS tests Listening and Speaking only, for citizenship.

CELPIP comes in two versions. CELPIP-General (IRCC calls it CELPIP-G) tests Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking in about 3 hours and is the one for Express Entry, work permits and Australian visas. CELPIP-General LS tests Listening and Speaking only, in a little over an hour, and is accepted for Canadian citizenship. $299 and $199 plus tax.

Practice the two sections both tests share. CELPIP Listening practice and CELPIP Speaking practice run on the real timers, with AI scoring on every Speaking answer. Taking the full General test? Run a complete timed mock first.

What is the difference between CELPIP General and CELPIP LS?

CELPIP General covers all four skills. CELPIP General LS drops Reading and Writing and keeps only Listening and Speaking. The Listening and Speaking sections themselves are the same in both tests: same parts, same timing, same scoring. What changes is which applications will accept the result.

IRCC's name for itCELPIP-GCELPIP-G LS
Skills testedListening, Reading, Writing, SpeakingListening, Speaking

Pick by the application, not by preference. If you need the test for anything other than citizenship, LS is not an option, however good your Listening and Speaking are.

What does CELPIP-G mean, and what is the full form of CELPIP?

CELPIP stands for Canadian English Language Proficiency Index Program. The G is General, so CELPIP-G is simply IRCC's short name for the CELPIP-General test. LS stands for Listening and Speaking, so CELPIP-G LS (also written CELPIP-General LS) is the two-skill version.

You will see both spellings in official places. Prometric, which owns CELPIP (it acquired the test's maker, Paragon Testing Enterprises, in 2021), writes "CELPIP-General" on its own site. IRCC's citizenship application guide writes "Canadian English Language Proficiency Index Program General Test (CELPIP-G) or the CELPIP-General LS (listening and speaking)", in Guide CIT 0002. Same test, two labels.

There is no CELPIP-G test that is different from CELPIP-General. If a website or an agent tells you to "take the G test" for PR, they mean CELPIP-General.

Which CELPIP test do I need for PR and Express Entry?

CELPIP-General, and only CELPIP-General. IRCC's Express Entry page says it plainly: "You must take the CELPIP-General test." An LS result cannot be entered in an Express Entry profile because the profile needs a CLB (Canadian Language Benchmark) level in all four skills.

The minimum level depends on the program you are applying under. These are the floors from IRCC's Express Entry language page, updated June 2026:

ProgramMinimum in English
Federal Skilled WorkerCLB 7 in all four skills
Canadian Experience Class, TEER 0 or 1 jobCLB 7 in all four skills
Canadian Experience Class, TEER 2 or 3 jobCLB 5 in all four skills
Federal Skilled TradesCLB 5 in Speaking and Listening, CLB 4 in Reading and Writing

A floor is not a target. Your CRS (Comprehensive Ranking System) points keep climbing well past the minimum, and CELPIP scores map to CLB levels one to one, so a 9 on the test is a CLB 9 on your file. The full points table is on our page on CELPIP scores for Express Entry and CRS points.

One more rule from the same IRCC page: your result must be less than two years old when you submit your profile and again when you submit the PR application. And there is no separate "immigration edition" of the test. The CELPIP-General you book through Prometric at celpip.ca is the one IRCC means.

Which CELPIP test do I need for Canadian citizenship?

Either one. IRCC accepts CELPIP-General and CELPIP-General LS as proof of language for citizenship, and it only looks at your Speaking and Listening scores. If you are 18 to 54, you need CLB 4 or higher in both, per IRCC's citizenship language proof page. Under 18 or 55 and over, you do not need to prove language at all.

If you already took CELPIP-General for your PR application, you probably do not need a new test. Guide CIT 0002 states that if you completed the test in the past for immigration or citizenship purposes, IRCC will accept the previous results even if they have expired. Check your old report: a 4 or higher in Speaking and Listening is enough.

If you never took CELPIP, or your old scores are below 4, the LS test is the cheaper route at $199 plus tax. What counts as a safe score, and how the Speaking part is judged, is on our page on the CELPIP score for Canadian citizenship.

Can I use CELPIP LS for a work permit or for Australia?

No, for both. A post-graduation work permit (PGWP) application made on or after November 1, 2024 needs a language result in all four skills: CLB 7 if you graduated from a university degree program, CLB 5 if you graduated from a college or other non-university program, per IRCC's PGWP eligibility page. A two-skill LS result cannot meet that.

Australia is the same story. The Department of Home Affairs accepts CELPIP General for visa applications, but only for tests taken at a secure test centre on or after 7 August 2025, and it lists "CELPIP Online" as not accepted, on its English language requirements page. LS is not on the list.

So the rule of thumb: citizenship is the one door LS opens. Every other door needs General.

CELPIP-G or CELPIP-General 2014: which one do I choose in the IRCC form?

Both labels describe the same test, and IRCC has not published an instruction on which to click. Here is what the two names mean, so you can choose without guessing.

Since April 1, 2014, Paragon has run only one version of the General test, which IRCC's own 2014 notice calls the "CELPIP General 2014" test, in this operational update. The older test that existed before that date is gone. Meanwhile IRCC's current citizenship guide calls the same current test "CELPIP-G." So a report card issued any time in the last twelve years is, in IRCC's own words, both a CELPIP-G and a CELPIP-General 2014.

What matters is that the scores you type match your report card exactly, skill by skill. The dropdown label does not change your CLB levels. Many applicants report choosing CELPIP-G without any problem, but that is applicant experience, not an IRCC rule, so treat it as such.

How long is the CELPIP LS test?

Listening takes 46 to 55 minutes and Speaking takes 15 minutes, so the LS test runs a little over an hour, done in one sitting at the test centre with no separate speaking appointment. Prometric rounds this to about 1 hour in the Guidebook for Test Takers and to about 70 minutes on its test format page at celpip.ca. Both figures are the same two sections added together.

The full General test adds Reading and Writing and runs about 3 hours in one sitting. Section by section timings, part names and question counts are on our full CELPIP test format guide.

Does CELPIP have an academic version?

Not any more. CELPIP-Academic was retired in August 2015, according to IRCC's designated test notice. Today there are exactly two CELPIP tests, General and General LS, and both measure everyday English rather than academic English.

That is the practical difference from IELTS, which still splits into Academic and General Training. If a Canadian college or a licensing body asks you for CELPIP, it means CELPIP-General. If you want the full comparison, start with what CELPIP is and how it is scored.

How much do CELPIP General and CELPIP LS cost?

In Canada, CELPIP-General is $299 plus tax and CELPIP-General LS is $199 plus tax, read from the live booking list at celpip.ca and confirmed on August 28, 2026. Tax is added on top and depends on the province, so the total in Ontario is higher than the total in Alberta for the same test.

Two things to know before you book. Prometric changes fees without much notice, so read the price beside the actual test date when you book, not on an information page. And the fee is per attempt: there is no discount for a retake and no partial refund for a section you did well on. Cancellation and rescheduling rules are on our page on CELPIP fees and refunds in 2026.

Is CELPIP LS easier than CELPIP General?

Shorter, not easier. The Listening section in LS is the same six-part Listening section as in General, and the Speaking section is the same eight tasks with the same timers and the same rating. You skip Reading and Writing, which saves you about two hours in the chair, but the two sections you do sit are not simplified.

In my experience the people who struggle on LS are the ones who assumed "only two sections" meant "light." Speaking still gives you 30 or 60 seconds to plan and 60 or 90 seconds to talk, task after task, and Listening still plays every clip once. Prepare for LS exactly the way you would prepare for those two sections of General.

How do I prepare for CELPIP General LS?

Practise the two sections under the real timers, and get your Speaking answers scored. Reading and Writing are off the table, so all of your prep time goes to Listening and Speaking, which is a real advantage if you use it.

For Listening, work through the six part types until you know what each one asks for, then do full timed sets on our CELPIP Listening practice page. For Speaking, record answers to all eight tasks on the CELPIP Speaking practice page and read the AI feedback on each one, because the score you are chasing for citizenship is a 4, and the fastest way to lose it is to run out of things to say at 30 seconds. If you are sitting the full General test, do the same two sections plus a full timed mock so the three-hour sitting is not a surprise on test day.

Frequently Asked Questions

1

Is CELPIP General accepted for citizenship, or do I have to take LS?

General is accepted. IRCC only looks at your Speaking and Listening scores, and a past General result is accepted even if it has expired, provided it was used for an earlier immigration or citizenship application.

2

Can I switch from LS to General after booking?

Only by booking the General test. The two are separate registrations, and LS scores cannot be topped up with Reading and Writing later.

3

Which CELPIP test do I need for a professional license?

CELPIP-General. Prometric lists professional designation among the uses of the General test. LS is accepted for citizenship only.

4

Do I choose CELPIP-G or CELPIP-General 2014 in the Express Entry profile?

They are the same test. IRCC has not published which label to pick; enter your scores exactly as printed on your report.
