## 1. Executive Summary & Pedagogical Vision

The **French A2 Learning Roadmap & Checklist** is a comprehensive, 71-page curriculum and self-assessment roadmap designed for upper-beginner to elementary French learners transitioning from CEFR A1 to full A2 mastery. 

While A1 focuses on **survival communication** (introducing oneself, basic needs, concrete immediate surroundings), A2 establishes **functional independence** in everyday life:
- Narrating stories, memories, and past events with the dual-past system (*passé composé* vs. *imparfait*).
- Projecting into the future (*futur simple* vs. *futur proche*).
- Formulating polite requests, wishes, and advice (*conditionnel de politesse*).
- Navigating multi-turn real-world interactions (housing, health, travel disruptions, administration, work).
- Using relative and object pronouns (*COD, COI, y, en, qui, que, où*) to avoid repetition and build fluid speech.
- Reaching a working vocabulary threshold of **1,500–2,000 words**.

The document is formatted to match the exact visual design language, typographical elegance, modular layout, and checkable ergonomics of the A1 roadmap.

### Module 2: A2 Grammar Foundation Master Checklist (Pages 8–19)
- **Page 8 (Nouns, Gender Exceptions & Plural Patterns)**:
  * Irregular plurals (*-al → -aux*, *-eau → -eaux*, invariant nouns).
  * Compound nouns and abstract noun gender recognition (*-tion, -té, -isme, -ment*).
- **Page 9 (Articles, Partitives & Quantifiers)**:
  * Omission of articles after negation (*pas de*) and quantity expressions (*beaucoup de, un kilo de*).
  * Definite articles for general truths vs. partitive articles for unspecified quantities.
- **Page 10 (Direct & Indirect Object Pronouns — COD & COI)**:
  * Direct object pronouns (*me, te, le, la, nous, vous, les*).
  * Indirect object pronouns (*me, te, lui, nous, vous, leur*).
  * Placement in simple vs. compound tenses vs. infinitive constructions.
- **Page 11 (Adverbial Pronouns 'Y', 'En' & Relative Pronouns)**:
  * Pronoun **Y** (replacing *à + lieu / chose*).
  * Pronoun **En** (replacing *de + chose*, indefinite quantities, numbers).
  * Relative pronouns: **qui** (subject), **que** (direct object), and **où** (place/time).
- **Page 12 (Adjectives, Comparison & Superlatives)**:
  * BANGS adjectives (Beauty, Age, Number, Goodness, Size) before the noun.
  * Meaning-changing adjectives by placement (*ancien, cher, propre, grand*).
  * Comparatives and superlatives (*plus/moins/aussi... que*, *le meilleur, le pire*).
- **Page 13 (Complex Negation & Adverbs)**:
  * Structures: *ne... jamais, ne... plus, ne... rien, ne... personne, ne... que*.
  * Placement rules with *passé composé* (*rien/jamais* before participle vs. *personne* after).
  * Formation of adverbs in *-ment* from adjectives.
- **Page 14 (Question Formation & Interrogative Structures)**:
  * Three registers: Informal (intonation), Standard (*est-ce que*), Formal (inversion).
  * Interrogative pronouns: *lequel, laquelle, lesquels, lesquelles* (introductory use).
  * Indirect questions (*Je voudrais savoir si / où...*).
- **Page 15 (Prepositions of Time, Space & Logical Connectors)**:
  * Prepositions of time: *depuis* (since/for), *pendant* (during), *dans* (in X time), *en* (duration), *pour* (intended duration).
  * Geographic prepositions (*en, au, aux, à* with cities/countries).
  * Sequencing connectors: *d'abord, ensuite, puis, alors, cependant, par conséquent, en effet*.
- **Page 16 (The Dual-Past System: Passé Composé Deep Dive)**:
  * Verbs taking *être* (DR & MRS VANDERTRAMP + all reflexive verbs).
  * Past participle agreement rules with *être* and preceding direct objects with *avoir*.
  * Expressing single completed actions and chronological sequences.
- **Page 17 (The Dual-Past System: L'Imparfait & Interrupted Past)**:
  * Formation of imparfait (regular stem from *nous* present tense + *-ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient*).
  * Uses: Descriptions, habitual actions, continuous background, physical/emotional states.
  * Interrupted actions: *Quand + imparfait, passé composé* ("Je dormais quand il est arrivé").
- **Page 18 (Futur Simple & Future Intentions)**:
  * Regular stems (*-er, -ir, -re*) and essential irregular stems (*aur-, ser-, ir-, fer-, saur-, pourr-, devr-, voudr-, viendr-*).
  * Contrasting *Futur Proche* (immediate/spoken) vs. *Futur Simple* (formal/long-term/predictions).
  * Time markers: *demain, l'année prochaine, dans deux jours, plus tard*.
- **Page 19 (Conditionnel de Politesse, Impératif & Modals)**:
  * Conditional present for courtesy (*Je voudrais, pourriez-vous, j'aimerais*).
  * Imperative with pronouns (*Parle-lui ! / Ne lui parle pas !*, *Vas-y !*).
  * Modal verbs: *devoir* (duty/necessity), *pouvoir* (permission/capacity), *falloir* (*il faut*).
