## Page 2

The A1 level is the
foundation
 of French learning, where the primary
goal is
survival communication
. Learners at this level are expected
to understand and use
very basic expressions
, interact in
everyday
situations
, and convey
personal information
 with limited
vocabulary and simple sentence structures.
By the end of A1, a learner should be able to:
Speak
Greet others and introduce themselves
Give basic personal information (name, age, nationality, job, hobbies)
Ask and answer simple questions (e.g., "Where do you live?", "What
time is it?", "What do you like?")
Talk about their daily routine, family, and immediate surroundings
Handle simple, rehearsed conversations in shops, cafés, and public
places
Overall Objective of A1 Level (Beginner)
